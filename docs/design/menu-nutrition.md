# Menu details: descriptions, ingredients, allergens and nutrition

Zak's menu poster (28 October 2026) lists twelve sizzling plates with their prices only.
The details the kiosk shows for each dish were prepared as follows. **All of them are
estimates to be confirmed with the owner**, who knows the actual recipes and portions.

## Descriptions and ingredients

Each dish's description and ingredients follow the usual Filipino recipe for it:

- Sisig: chopped grilled pork with onion, chili, calamansi, soy sauce and mayonnaise,
  topped with an egg ([Panlasang Pinoy](https://panlasangpinoy.com/wprm_print/49015),
  [Bite My Bun](https://www.bitemybun.com/pork-sisig-recipe-sizzling-pork-sisig/)).
- Chicken pecho: an inasal-style marinade of calamansi, vinegar, garlic, lemongrass and
  ginger, basted with annatto oil ([Kawaling Pinoy](https://www.kawalingpinoy.com/wprm_print/chicken-inasal)).
- Chicken BBQ: soy sauce, banana ketchup, calamansi, garlic, brown sugar and lemon-lime
  soda ([Panlasang Pinoy](https://panlasangpinoy.com/wprm_print/48004)).
- Bangus: boneless milkfish marinated in vinegar and garlic, then fried
  ([Manila Spoon](https://www.manilaspoon.com/daing-na-bangus-milkfish-marinated-in/)).
- Calamares: squid rings coated in flour, cornstarch and egg, then fried
  ([Bite My Bun](https://www.bitemybun.com/filipino-calamares-recipe-fried-squid-rings/)).
- Hungarian sausage: pork, beef, soy protein and milk solids, as on a local brand's label
  ([Delimondo](https://delimondo.ph/products/hungarian-sausage)).
- Every plate: java rice fried in margarine with annatto
  ([Panlasang Pinoy](https://panlasangpinoy.com/java-rice-recipe/)) and a brown gravy of
  butter, flour, beef broth and soy sauce
  ([Panlasang Pinoy](https://panlasangpinoy.com/sizzling-pork-chop-recipe/)).

## Allergens

Listed generously, since a missing allergen is worse than an extra one:

- **Every plate:** Gluten, Milk and Soy, from the gravy (flour, butter, soy sauce) and the
  margarine in the java rice.
- **Added per dish:** Egg (sisig, fried chicken, chicken fillet, calamares), Fish
  (bangus), Shellfish (calamares: squid is a mollusc), Sesame (chicken teriyaki).
